Comments to the Author:\
Based on interviews with 19 “lay users” (patients?) and 7 medical professionals in China, this paper explores how they have used Ant A-Fu, an AI-based medical diagnosis application, and how they validated its informatio, how they used it during clinical encounters, and how they handle conflicts due to the misalignment with clinicians’ judgments, etc. Based on the findings, the paper provides some design implications, to some degree, to support the design of AI-generated health information applications.\
\
Overall, reviewers found this paper as “well-written” (R2), with a “clear motivation” (R2), addressing an “important, timely, and relevant” topic for CSCW research (R1, R3), human-centred health informatics (R3), and aligned with “ongoing research within the broader HCI community” (R2). While reviewers recognise the potential of this paper to make a strong and valuable contribution to CSCW research and beyond, all reviewers raised many issues and concerns that need to be addressed before publication.\
\
All reviewer’s recommendations pointed out to major revisions. I agree with all the revisions requested and we are giving the authors the opportunity to restructure their paper to address the major revisions requested by the reviewers. Reviewers will look at the revised manuscript one more time but please be aware that considering the scope of the revisions this paper is unlikely to be accepted unless all or most of the reviewers concerns are addressed in the new version to improve the quality of the contribution to the CSCW community.\
\
Here, a summary of the major issues:\
\
a) Clarity in the presentation of the conceptual contribution, framing and related work:\
It seems that the findings of this paper wander around many different concepts that are not properly defined and made it difficult for the reviewers to “identify a sufficiently coherent central idea” (R1) that connect all the different sections of the paper. While R1 listed many of these concepts (e.g., trust calibration, selective visibility, coordination work, accountability), R3 in particularly requested to explicitly define “epistemic authority” in relation to previous CSCW/HCI and social sciences research. R1 also suggested to expand the framing of the paper to consider the “broader sociotechnical phenomenon” that could help better articulate the contribution of this paper and connect all the different section with clearer “conceptual insights”.\
\
To help with this reframing, R1 also suggested changes in the related work and avoid high-level summaries of previous work and to engage more deeply and critically with the results “e.g., tensions, limitations, unresolved questions” from previous work. In addition, R2 also provides an example of missing previous work--see below.\
\
These changes will help to better highlight the research gap, novelty and the new knowledge contribution throughout the paper.  \
\
b) Clarity in the presentation of the research context, methods, analysis, and results:\
One of the major issues highlighted both by R2 and R3 relates to the lack of details in description of the methods and results in relation to the involvement of the medical professionals. I agree that more details are needed regarding the procedures e.g., Were they asked the same or different questions? (R2). In addition, R3 asked to clearly distinguish in the findings what insights are coming from the different participants (experts and lay users) rather than using a generic “user” terminology. Also, R1 asked for clarifications regarding the patient’s role of these lay users. I agree and urge the authors to clarify this throughout the paper as the abstract mentions “patients” but the methods used the term “lay users” and the Table 1 does not show any characteristic that could describe their patient role. R1 also asked to make more explicitly the sampling strategy for participant recruitment.\
\
R2 also asked for including more details regarding the application and its features, which is basically the focus of this paper and highlighting this will provide more context for reviewers to properly assess the results of this paper. In addition, R3 also asked for additional details about the Chinese healthcare/institutional context that could help to understand the “selective visibility”.\
\
Another issue seems to be the codebook thematic analysis and the four identified themes. Both R2 and R3 found them difficult to understand as they were never defined nor explained. In addition, is it possible to include a reference to the method for analysis used and provide details regarding any validity process that was followed to ensure reliability or demonstrate rigour?  \
\
The presentation of the findings seems to be a problem as well. R1 asked to clear distinguish findings that relate to the health or medical context from the ones that reflect “general AI-use practices”. In addition, R1 found lack of “analytical depth and novelty” in the findings as some of them might have been reported in previous work. Also, R2 reported that Figure 3 and Figure 4 needs more elaboration as they seem disconnected from the results.\
\
R3 also asked for a clear statement regarding ethical approval for the study and to reflect on the limitations of the methods used. We urge the authors to made this clarification if any formal review process was in place.  \

It will be handled by the same Associate Editor and set of reviewers.: This paper investigates how users of Ant A-Fu, a medical AI application, evaluate, verify, communicate, and act upon AI-generated health information. Major Concerns:\
1\. Lack of a coherent conceptual spine. I found it difficult to identify a sufficiently coherent central idea that organizes these findings into a cumulative argument. The manuscript moves among trust calibration, multi-source verification, selective visibility, coordination work, trilateral patient–AI–physician relationships, epistemic authority, decision-making responsibility, and accountability. These concepts are all relevant, but their relationships remain under-theorized. Consequently, the paper sometimes reads as a sequence of related findings about medical AI use rather than an account of a clearly defined overarching phenomenon.\
I also found the overall framing of the manuscript somewhat too system-specific. For example, the four RQs somewhat too narrowly centered on Ant A-Fu as a specific system. As a result, especially in the first half, the manuscript sometimes reads more like a user study of Ant A-Fu than an investigation of a broader sociotechnical phenomenon. This sits somewhat uneasily with the later findings and discussion, which make broader claims about patient-facing medical AI, patient–clinician communication, epistemic authority, distributed informational work, and clinical accountability. I encourage the authors to clarify the broader phenomenon that Ant A-Fu serves as an empirical case for, and to establish this framing earlier in the manuscript. Doing so would help align the Introduction, RQs, findings, and discussion, while also making clearer what conceptual insights from this case may travel beyond Ant A-Fu itself.



1\. 缺少连贯的核心概念主线（coherent conceptual spine）：我很难识别出足够清晰统一的中心思想，能够把各项研究发现组织成层层递进的完整论证。稿件同时涉及信任校准、多源核验、选择性可见性、协调劳动、患者‑AI‑医生三方关系、认知权威、决策责任、问责等诸多概念。这些概念均具备相关性，但概念之间的理论推演与关联分析做得不足。因此，读论文的时候，更像是一组相互有关联的关于医疗 AI 使用的现象罗列，而非围绕某个界定清晰的宏观社会技术现象展开的完整研究。

同时，整篇稿件的整体框架过分绑定特定系统。例如，四个研究问题过于局限于安福（Ant A‑Fu）这一个具体产品。结果就是，稿件前半部分读起来更像是安福这款产品的用户研究，而不是对某一更加宽泛的社会技术现象开展的探究。但论文后半部分的结果与讨论又会面向面向患者的医疗 AI、患医沟通、认知权威、分布式信息劳动、临床问责提出普适性论断，二者之间存在不协调。

我建议作者明确：安福是作为实证案例用来研究哪一个更宏大的现象，并且在论文靠前位置就建立这套分析框架。这样做可以让引言、研究问题、研究结果、讨论部分逻辑对齐，同时清晰说明从这个案例中提炼出的哪些概念洞见可以迁移到安福产品之外的场景。

5\. The Findings contain rich empirical material, but I think they could benefit from stronger analytical synthesis. At present, the organization closely follows the four RQs, which makes the findings somewhat procedural and, at times, descriptive. 讨论章节需要进一步的概念整合与理论拓展

以 5.2.1 小节《临床工作流中的三方关系》为例，它的概念新颖性还需要更有说服力的论证。

仅仅指出患‑医交互中新增 AI 这一行动者 / 信息来源，本身并不构成有力的概念贡献。真正有价值的是：这一变化如何重塑临床沟通组织方式、认知权威分配、协调劳动以及问责机制。

该小节论证推进也有待加强：开篇抛出 “三方关系” 这一大论断，后续却分散到一个个零散观察，没有逐步构建、夯实这套概念论证。目前 “三方关系” 更多只是一个描述性标签，而不是一个充分发展的分析性概念。
